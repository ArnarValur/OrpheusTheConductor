# Rename TheOracle to Orpheus at v3.0

> **Recorded:** 2026-07-11 00:42
> **Status:** accepted

With Merkurial-studio's migration from Google Antigravity to the Claude ecosystem, the domain is renamed TheOracle → Orpheus. Version lineage continues (v2.1 → v3.0) rather than resetting to v1.0: the major bump marks the re-platforming while preserving the v2.0 → v2.1 migration history and precedent. Source headers become `# Source: Orpheus v3.0 @ <date>`.

## Considered Options

- Orpheus v1.0 (fresh lineage) — cleaner brand, but orphans the migration history.
- Orpheus v2.2 (minimal rename) — undersells a re-platforming as a patch.
